When we asked an AI search tool about storm damage in Lakeview, it named Lakeview Roofing as the strongest local option. Ask the same about hail claims in Fairhaven, Westbrook or Pine Hill and it names Crestline, Northpoint Builders, Harbor Restoration and Summit Exteriors instead. These are indicative checks, not Google rankings. AI answers cite pages, reviews and listings; today you have no roofing or storm pages for those towns, few recent reviews, and listings that disagree. The new site gives each town and service its own page with an FAQ and schema, and around it we grow reviews, match every listing, and do local SEO, Google Business Profile work and AI-answer setup (including llms.txt). We’ll repeat these same checks to measure progress.
