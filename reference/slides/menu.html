As we discussed, look at your menu. Services, Before and After, Reviews and Contact don’t open pages: they jump down to a section of the homepage. Only Service Areas and About are real pages. Google ranks pages, not sections, so a homeowner searching for roof repair or reviews lands on a long homepage instead of a page built for that question, and AI answers have nothing specific to quote. On the new site every link in the menu goes to its own page, each with its own title, FAQ and schema. [Service note: Website Design and SEO, both on northwind.example.]
