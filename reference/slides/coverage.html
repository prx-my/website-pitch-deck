You serve seven towns and five kinds of work: that’s 35 combinations a homeowner might search, like “roof repair Westbrook” or “kitchen remodel Pine Hill”. Your sitemap covers 10 of them. Roofing pages exist only for Lakeview and Fairhaven; the other five towns only have siding-repair pages; and there are no town pages for remodeling, gutters or windows, even though remodeling is in your company name. There’s also a Stonebridge page, which is in another county, far outside the lakeshore, and two pages recruiting roofing subcontractors next to your “no subcontractor roulette” promise. The new site gives every town and service its own page, each with a local FAQ and schema, and drops the off-area and recruiting pages.
